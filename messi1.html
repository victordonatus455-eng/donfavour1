About Messi

This is a brief introduction to Lionel Messi, the famous Argentine footballer.

Lionel Messi is an Argentine professional footballer who captains both Inter Miami and the Argentina national team, and holds a record eight Ballon d'Or awards.

Fact that messi is better than ronaldo

  • Goals Per Game: Messi holds a higher career scoring average of 0.79 goals per match, compared to Ronaldo's 0.73 goals per match.
  • Assists: Messi has recorded over 418 career assists, establishing a massive gap over Ronaldo's 261 assists.
  • Goal Contributions: Because of his heavy assist numbers, Messi leads in total direct goal contributions (goals + assists combined) despite playing over 160 fewer career games than Ronaldo.
  • Goal Ratios: According to career data compiled by The Week, Messi maintains a higher overall career goal-per-game ratio than Ronaldo.
  • Ballon d'Or Awards: Messi has won eight Ballon d'Or trophies, while Ronaldo has won five.
